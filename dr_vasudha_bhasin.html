Yes, regular dental visits are essential. They help prevent major oral health issues, detect problems early, and maintain healthy teeth and gums. Most dentists recommend a visit every 6 months.
Modern periodontal treatments are performed with advanced techniques and anaesthesia to ensure maximum patient comfort. Dr. Vasudha focuses on pain-managed, stress-free procedures for every patient.
Professional teeth cleaning is generally recommended every 6 months. However, patients with gum disease or higher plaque accumulation may need more frequent visits — Dr. Vasudha will recommend what's best for you.
